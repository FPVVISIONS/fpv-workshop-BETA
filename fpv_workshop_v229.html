FPV Workshop

Build a 3.5″ FPV quad from bare parts. Solder it, configure it, plug in the battery… and hope it doesn’t let the magic smoke out.

Choose difficulty
EasyGuided builderA checklist, hints and a coach tell you what you missed or got wrong. If the test fails, it tells you why.
NormalFind your own mistakesNo checklist and no warnings. You only learn something is wrong when you plug the battery in: smoke, a dead motor or a crash. You get a short symptom.
HardReal benchNo HUD, no coach, no notes, no messages. Only what you see and hear: smoke, silence, the startup tones, the flight.
🔊 Turn your sound on. A healthy quad plays two BLHeli_32 startup tones when the battery goes in. If you only hear one, something is wrong.
